line racer is an open-source Python package for the rapid calculation of high-resolution radiative opacities for exoplanet atmospheres. The paper is available as a [preprint on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33290) and is currently under review at JOSS; the [documentation is available here](https://line-racer.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).
