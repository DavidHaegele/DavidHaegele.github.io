I am a PhD student at the [Max Planck Institute for Astronomy](https://www.mpia.de/) in Heidelberg, where I study the atmospheres of exoplanets.

My research focuses on the modelling of exoplanet atmospheres and the radiative opacities needed to interpret their spectra. I develop [line racer](https://line-racer.readthedocs.io/en/latest/), an open source package for the rapid calculation of high-resolution opacities, and I am part of the development team of [petitRADTRANS](https://petitradtrans.readthedocs.io/en/latest/).

On this site you can find my [publications](/publications/), the [software](/software/) I work on, and my [CV](/cv/). When I am not doing science, I am probably riding my bike up a mountain — see the [cycling](/cycling/) page.

Feel free to reach out by [email](mailto:haegele@mpia.de)!
